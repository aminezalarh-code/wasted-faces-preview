Mahmood Shubbar

Founder of Shubbarialism

Iraqi master painter and founder of Shubbarialism, a visual language that theatricalises fine art. His Antara and Abla series stages the legendary knight-poet among red bears, microphones and cinema seats, linking times and inviting the viewer to interact rather than evaluate.

Works are offered privately. Prices on request.
